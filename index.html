I’m a master’s student at the Robotics Institute at Carnegie Mellon University, where I work with David Held in the Robots Perceiving and Doing Lab.

I work on robot manipulation, developing policies that adapt by learning from their own history of actions and outcomes.

Before CMU, I studied computer science at NYU Shanghai and worked on visual imitation learning for robot manipulation with Lerrel Pinto at NYU.

Scouting the Dynamics Gap: Test-Time Policy Adaptation via Action-Outcome Feedback

Liyuan Geng*, Yishu Li*, Xinyi Mao*, Amber Li, David Held

SCOUT adapts a robot’s manipulation policy to unfamiliar dynamics using the gap between predicted and observed action outcomes.
